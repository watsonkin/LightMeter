支持

测光表是一款面向胶片与手动相机用户的单机测光 App。用 iPhone 估算场景亮度，给出可直接拨到相机上的光圈或快门建议。

  • 无需账号、无需联网即可测光
  • ISO、快门优先 / 光圈优先、曝光补偿

Support

LightMeter is an offline exposure meter for film and manual cameras. It estimates scene brightness on your iPhone and suggests shutter or aperture values you can dial in.

  • No account, no network required for metering
  • ISO, shutter / aperture priority, exposure compensation
  • Optional tips via Apple In-App Purchase — tips do not unlock features
